From enrolment to certificate

  1. 1. Enrol

    Pick a free or paid course, or subscribe monthly or yearly for access to everything included in your plan.

  2. 2. Learn

    Work through lessons at your pace, join live classes, ask the AI study assistant, and message your teacher.

  3. 3. Be assessed

    Submit assignments, take quizzes, and sit timed examinations with auto-save if your connection drops.

  4. 4. Get certified

    When you meet the course requirements the certificate is issued automatically. Download it as a PDF.
